## Context
What problem is being decided? What forces are in play (operational, schema, performance, caller ergonomics)? Frame the situation; do not pre-bias the decision. Keep it short — one or two paragraphs is usually enough.

## Decision
The single sentence that captures the call, followed by enough detail to make it concrete (config flag names, schema columns, code structure pointers). This section is the load-bearing part of the document.

## Consequences
- Direct effects on operators, callers, contributors, and the codebase.
- New invariants the system must uphold (with the test or constraint that pins them, where applicable).
- Specific things that get harder, not just things that get easier.

## Alternatives considered
- **Alternative A.** Why rejected — be specific. "Adds complexity" is too vague.
- **Alternative B.** Same.

## Revisit if
The concrete change in circumstances that would justify reopening this decision. ADRs are immutable; this section is how the future learns what would invalidate the present.
